      Terms and policy

      Sample policy text: replace it with your official terms.

      Payment deadlines and instalments

      Fees are due before registration closes. Students may pay in 3 instalments: 50% at registration, 30% by week 5 and 20% by week 9.

      Late payment

      Fees paid after the deadline attract a late fee of KES 2,000. Exam cards are released only after the required instalment is cleared.

      Refunds and withdrawals

      Withdrawing in the first 2 weeks earns a 75% refund. After week 2 fees are not refundable. Refunds are paid within 30 days to the original payer.

      Module availability

      A module marked Full cannot be registered. If a module is cancelled, fees are moved to another module or refunded in full.

      Scholarships and bursaries

      Apply to the finance office within the first 3 weeks. Awards are reviewed each semester.

      Privacy of financial records

      Your financial record is only shared with you and authorised finance staff.
